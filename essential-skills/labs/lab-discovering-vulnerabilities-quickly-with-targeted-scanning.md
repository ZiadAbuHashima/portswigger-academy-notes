# Lab: Discovering vulnerabilities quickly with targeted scanning

**Official PortSwigger URL:** [https://portswigger.net/web-security/essential-skills/using-burp-scanner-during-manual-testing/lab-discovering-vulnerabilities-quickly-with-targeted-scanning](https://portswigger.net/web-security/essential-skills/using-burp-scanner-during-manual-testing/lab-discovering-vulnerabilities-quickly-with-targeted-scanning)

**Lab idea:** This challenge practices using Burp Scanner selectively during manual reconnaissance. The learner must investigate a server-side file-reading vulnerability under a time limit, emphasizing how to prioritize a promising endpoint and use scanner findings to guide further analysis rather than scan the entire site indiscriminately.

## Vulnerability in brief
**Essential skills** is a general web-security learning and testing category, not a vulnerability class. It covers transferable methods for applying Academy knowledge to less predictable targets, making efficient use of Burp features during manual testing, and learning to recognize vulnerabilities without being told in advance which class to seek. The Academy also emphasizes that labs demonstrate only particular variations of bugs; real testing requires adapting to differences in how applications process input.
